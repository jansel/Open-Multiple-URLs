# Open Multiple URLs Tab privacy policy

Effective October 25, 2026.

Open Multiple URLs Tab processes the text you type or paste locally in your browser. The developer does not collect, receive, sell, or share your text, URLs, browsing history, or personal information. The extension contains no analytics, advertising, tracking, or remote code.

## Local storage

Your opening preferences are saved using Chrome's local extension storage. **Preserve input**, enabled by default, also saves the input text in that storage so it can be restored when the workspace is reloaded or reopened. Turning this option off clears the saved input; it leaves the current tab's text available until you close or reload it. Uninstalling the extension removes its local storage.

The extension does not use Chrome sync storage. Separate devices and browser profiles keep separate input and settings. If you explicitly allow the extension in incognito mode, Chrome's local extension storage is shared between normal and incognito use within that profile.

## Opening links and searches

When you click **Open URLs**, your browser visits the destinations you requested. Those websites receive the normal information associated with a browser visit and have their own privacy policies. Lazy loading postpones supported visits until their tabs are selected.

When you enable **Handle Non-URLs as search queries**, input lines without a URL scheme are sent to your browser's default search provider when you click **Open URLs**. The provider's privacy policy applies to those searches.

Tab group information is used locally to let you select a group for newly opened tabs. It is not sent to the developer. The extension does not read unrelated web page content or monitor the clipboard; it handles only paste events in its own text field.
